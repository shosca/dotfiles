## name: unslop description: Cut AI tells from any writing. Must always apply.

# Unslop

Edit text to remove AI patterns while keeping the meaning and intended tone. Scan the exact final
text for the patterns below, rewrite, and check once more for anything that still sounds
machine-written.

## Word choice

- **AI vocabulary and puffery.** Replace words like `additionally`, `crucial`, `delve`, `enduring`,
  `enhance`, `foster`, `garner`, `interplay`, `intricate`, `landscape`, `pivotal`, `showcase`,
  `tapestry`, `testament`, `underscore`, `vibrant`, `load bearing`, `deeply rooted`,
  `groundbreaking`, `nestled`, and `stunning`. State plain facts.
- **Plain words.** Write `is` or `has` instead of `serves as`, `stands as`, `boasts`, or
  `features`. Prefer `use`, `help`, `many`, `if`, `to`, and `because` over `utilize`, `leverage`,
  `facilitate`, `numerous`, `in the event that`, `in order to`, and `due to the fact that`.
- **Filler and hedging.** Remove `it is important to note that` and collapse stacked hedges like
  `could potentially possibly` into `may`.
- **Abstract metaphors.** Replace metaphor nouns with concrete words: `substrate` (base), `wedge`
  (add), `vector` (way), `locus`, `vantage`, `nexus`, `primitive`, `harness`, `surface`, `bedrock`,
  `scaffolding`, `modality`, `paradigm`, `gold-plating`, `ratchet`, `evacuate` (move out),
  `endgame`, `north star`, and `flywheel`.
- **Mannered prose.** Remove aphorisms, rhetorical fragments, personified code, figurative verbs
  like `rides along` or `stands on`, and stock phrases like `a dial worth turning`.
- **Strong verbs and consistent terms.** Replace weak verb-plus-adverb pairs with a stronger verb
  or a number. Keep one name for the same concept instead of cycling synonyms.

## Substance

- **Say what it does, not how it feels.** Name the mechanism, command, or number. Remove any
  sentence that could appear unchanged in another project's docs.
- **Concrete claims.** Remove vague attributions (`experts believe`), uncontextualized media
  name-drops, superficial `-ing` clauses (`highlighting`, `ensuring`, `fostering`), formulaic
  challenges (`despite challenges, continues to thrive`), and generic conclusions (`the future looks bright`). Name the source or state the fact.
- **Direct structure.** State points directly instead of `not just X, but Y`. List topics directly
  instead of false `from X to Y` ranges. Use the natural number of items instead of forcing threes.

## Sentence craft

- **Active voice and short sentences.** Name the actor (`the compiler checks queries`, not passive
  voice) unless the actor is unknown or irrelevant. Split dense sentences to one idea per sentence.
- **No over-compression.** Write full sentences with articles and verbs. Spell out arrows and
  shorthand so the reader does not decode fragments.

## Formatting and punctuation

- **No em dashes.** Use periods or commas. Do not substitute en dashes, hyphens, or parentheses.
- **Colons.** Use colons before lists or examples only, never as mid-sentence connectors.
- **Headings, quotes, and emphasis.** Use sentence-case headings, straight quotes, and sparse
  boldface. Remove decorative emojis from headings and bullets.
- **Inline-header lists.** Convert bold-colon labels that restate the line (`**Performance:** Performance improved`) to prose. Bold lead-ins ending in a period with genuinely new detail are
  fine.

## Chatbot artifacts

Remove chatbot filler (`I hope this helps`, `Of course`, `Found the smoking gun`), sycophancy
(`Great question`), and cutoff disclaimers (`While specific details are limited`). Respond
directly.

## Voice

In essays or commentary, avoid sterile neutrality. Take a clear stance, acknowledge complexity,
vary sentence rhythm, use first person when it fits, and pick specific details over generic
phrasing.
