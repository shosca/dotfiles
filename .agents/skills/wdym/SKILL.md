name: wdym
description: Restate the user's goals and problem in plain words and confirm understanding.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

When invoked, restate in your own words what the user is trying to achieve and what problem they are trying to solve.

Format:
- **Goal:** what the user wants.
- **Problem:** what is wrong or missing today.
- **Assumptions / unknowns:** what you are unsure about.

Use 3–6 bullets total. Keep it short. Then ask: "Did I capture it? Anything to correct or add?"

Do not take action until the user confirms.
