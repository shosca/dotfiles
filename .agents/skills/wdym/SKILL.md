---
name: wdym
description: Restate the user's goals and problem in plain words and confirm understanding.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

When invoked, restate in your own words what the user is trying to achieve and what problem they are trying to solve and what you are unsure about.

Keep it short. Then ask: "Did I capture it? Anything to correct or add?"

Do not take action until the user confirms.
